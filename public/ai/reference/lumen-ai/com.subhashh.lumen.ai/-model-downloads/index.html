ModelDownloads

object ModelDownloads(source)

Starts, tracks and cancels model downloads. Each ModelPack is asked for on its own; a single transfer works through the queue (AiState.modelQueue) one pack after another. The transfer runs in a user-initiated data transfer job on Android 14+ (exempt from job quotas, survives leaving the app), and in a WorkManager foreground worker on Android 11–13. Both call run.
