OcrModel

object OcrModel(source)

Reading text in photos: Baidu's PP-OCRv6 small (Apache 2.0), text detection and recognition, 50 languages (Chinese, English, Japanese, Latin scripts), plus our GPU conversion of the detector (tools/ocr/; the repo also has GPU line readers, tried and left out: no faster on real lines). Downloaded with either Smart Search tier. rec.yml carries the recognizer's character list.
