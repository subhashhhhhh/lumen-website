LiveText

object LiveText(source)

Live Text: the text in the photo on screen, with where every character is, so it can be selected and copied. Text Reading keeps each photo's lines as it indexes (TextStore.lines), so an indexed photo has them straight away; others are read on demand at up to ReadSide, and kept the same way. The last few are also held in memory. The reader loads as the viewer opens (warm) and stays a little after it closes (release).
