SmartIndex

object SmartIndex(source)

Keeps Smart Search's index current, in the background, with nothing for the user to do:

  • Backlog (everything not indexed yet) runs only while charging and the battery isn't low, so a first index of thousands of photos and videos never costs battery; unless the user turns on Index on Battery (Settings → Smart Search), which drops the charger rule.

  • New items: MediaStore wakes Lumen shortly after a photo or video is added; up to NewLimit get indexed right away, charging or not. More than that waits for the charger.

  • Scheduled Indexing (Settings): the backlog runs only in the user's hours (overnight, say), plugged in or not; a small job wakes Lumen at the window's start and end. Both resume where they stopped. Scheduled at app start and when a model finishes downloading.
